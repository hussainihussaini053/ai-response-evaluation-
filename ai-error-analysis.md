# AI Error Analysis Sample

## Purpose

This sample demonstrates my ability to identify errors in AI-generated responses and provide clear human feedback for improvement.

## Example Task

**User instruction:**

Give three simple reasons why people should save money.

### AI Response

People should save money because it is always necessary to become wealthy. Saving money guarantees that a person will never experience financial problems. It also ensures that a person can buy anything they want whenever they want.

## Error Analysis

### 1. Factual Overstatement

The statement that saving money "guarantees" that a person will never experience financial problems is too absolute. Saving money can provide financial support, but it cannot guarantee that someone will never face financial difficulties.

### 2. Unsupported Claim

The response suggests that saving money ensures a person can buy anything they want. This is unrealistic because the amount a person saves may not be enough to purchase everything they want.

### 3. Instruction Quality

The user asked for three simple reasons. The response provides reasons, but some of them are exaggerated and should be expressed more accurately.

## Suggested Improved Response

People should save money for several reasons:

1. **Emergencies:** Savings can help cover unexpected expenses.
2. **Future plans:** Savings can help people prepare for important future needs.
3. **Financial security:** Having some money saved can reduce financial pressure when unexpected costs arise.

## Human Feedback

The original response contains ideas related to saving money, but some claims are too absolute and unrealistic. The response should avoid words such as "guarantees" when the information cannot be guaranteed. A better answer should use accurate, balanced, and simple language.

## Errors Identified

| Error Type | Issue | Recommended Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Factual overstatement | Saving guarantees financial stability | Use more accurate language |
| Unsupported claim | Saving allows someone to buy anything | Avoid unrealistic claims |
| Clarity | Some statements are too absolute | Use simple and balanced wording |
| Instruction quality | The answer could better match the user's simple request | Give three clear and realistic reasons |
